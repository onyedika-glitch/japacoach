## Full description (max 4000)
Hit your IELTS band before your test date.

Japa Coach is an AI IELTS practice app built in Nigeria for people preparing to study, work or settle abroad. Practise all four modules on your phone and get an estimated band score with specific fixes in minutes, not days.

SPEAKING, LIKE THE REAL TEST
• The examiner asks each question out loud and you answer with your voice
• Part 1, Part 2 (cue card with 1 minute to prepare) and Part 3, or a full speaking mock
• Get your estimated band for Fluency, Vocabulary, Grammar and Pronunciation
• See the exact sentences to fix, pronunciation tips and stronger versions of your answers
• Track words per minute, long pauses and filler words

WRITING, MARKED IN A MINUTE
• Task 1 charts, tables and letters, and Task 2 essays, for Academic and General Training
• Live word count and exam timer
• Band for each criterion, line by line corrections and a model answer

READING AND LISTENING
• Full 40 question practice tests with real exam question types
• True / False / Not Given, matching, gap fill and multiple choice
• Marked instantly with the correct answers and explanations
• Listening transcripts so you can see what you missed

FULL MOCK EXAMS AND A STUDY PLAN
• Take all four modules and get one overall band
• A week by week study plan built from your results, target band and test date
• Track your progress in every module over time

BUILT FOR YOU
• Works on everyday Android phones
• Start free with no card
• Upgrade with a weekly, monthly or 90 day pass through Google Play, with no automatic renewal
• Email reminders before your test date (you can turn them off)
• Delete your account and data at any time from Settings

Japa Coach gives estimated band scores based on the public IELTS band descriptors. It is an independent practice tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IELTS, the British Council, IDP or Cambridge University Press & Assessment.
