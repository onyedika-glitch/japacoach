## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your profile picture.
- **Study profile:** test type, target band and test date, which you choose to give us.
- **Your practice work:** speaking recordings, transcripts, essays and answers, plus the scores and feedback we generate.
- **Payments:** the plan you bought, the amount and the payment reference. Card details are handled by Bachs on the website and by Google Play in the Android app, never by us.

## How we use it

- To mark your answers and show your progress.
- To build your study plan and send reminders (you can turn reminders off in settings).
- To process payments and prevent fraud.

## AI processing

Speaking recordings are transcribed on our own servers. Transcripts and essays are sent to an AI language model provider to generate scores and feedback. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

**Audio recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days.** Transcripts, essays, scores and feedback stay in your history until you delete your account. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

## Age

Japa Coach is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices

You can update your profile at any time in settings. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time on our [delete account page](/delete-account) or in the mobile app (More, Settings, Delete my account). Deletion is immediate.

## Cookies

We use essential cookies only: to keep you signed in and to protect forms from abuse.

## Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us through the support details in your dashboard.
