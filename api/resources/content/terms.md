## 1. What Japa Coach is

Japa Coach is an independent online practice tool for the IELTS test. We use artificial intelligence to mark practice answers and suggest improvements. Japa Coach is **not affiliated with IELTS, the British Council, IDP or Cambridge University Press & Assessment**, and "IELTS" is used only to describe the test you are preparing for.

## 2. Band scores are estimates

Every band score we show is an estimate based on the public IELTS band descriptors. It is not an official result and does not guarantee the score you will get on test day. Do not rely on our estimates for visa, admission or employment decisions.
